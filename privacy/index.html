1. Overview

SingLift is a vocal stability instrument developed by Blue Arc Studio. The app measures voice steadiness in real time through your device's microphone.

This Privacy Policy explains how SingLift handles information when you use the app.

2. Developer

Developer: Blue Arc Studio

Contact: support@bluearcstudio.com

3. Microphone Access

SingLift requires microphone access to measure vocal stability in real time.

The microphone is used only for the core function of the app: analyzing voice input and displaying vocal stability feedback.

4. On-device Processing

Voice analysis is performed locally on your device.

SingLift does not send voice recordings or microphone audio to Blue Arc Studio servers or any external server.

5. Voice Data

SingLift does not collect, store, upload, or share voice recordings.

The app processes microphone input temporarily on your device to calculate real-time vocal stability feedback.

6. Account Information

SingLift does not require you to create an account.

We do not collect your name, email address, login information, or account credentials through the app.

7. Data Collection

The current version of SingLift does not collect personal data through the app.

The current version of SingLift does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cloud-based voice processing.

8. Data Sharing

SingLift does not sell personal data.

SingLift does not share voice recordings with third parties because voice recordings are not collected by the app.

9. Children

SingLift is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

SingLift is designed to process voice input locally on your device. Since the app does not upload voice recordings to Blue Arc Studio servers or any external server, voice recordings are not stored by Blue Arc Studio.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, data handling, or legal requirements change.

If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SingLift's privacy practices, please contact us by email.
