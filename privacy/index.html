Privacy Policy

1. Overview

SingLift is a vocal stability instrument developed by Blue Arc Studio. It is designed to help users observe voice steadiness in real time during focused vocal practice.

This Privacy Policy explains how SingLift handles information when you use the app.

2. Developer

Developer: Blue Arc Studio

Contact: support@bluearcstudio.com

3. Microphone Access

SingLift requires microphone access to observe vocal stability in real time.

The microphone is used only for the core function of the app: processing voice input locally and displaying real-time feedback.

4. On-device Processing

Voice analysis is processed locally on your device during use.

SingLift does not send microphone audio or voice recordings to Blue Arc Studio servers or to any external server for cloud-based analysis.

5. Voice Data

SingLift does not collect, store, upload, or share voice recordings.

Microphone input is processed temporarily on your device in order to provide real-time vocal stability feedback.

6. Account Information

SingLift does not require account creation.

The app does not collect your name, email address, login credentials, or account profile information through in-app registration.

7. Data Collection

The current version of SingLift does not collect personal data through the app.

The current version does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cloud-based voice processing.

8. Data Sharing

SingLift does not sell personal data.

SingLift does not share voice recordings with third parties because voice recordings are not collected by the app.

9. Children

SingLift is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

SingLift is designed so that voice processing happens locally on your device. Because microphone audio is not uploaded to Blue Arc Studio servers, voice recordings are not stored by Blue Arc Studio.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, data handling, or legal requirements change.

If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SingLift's privacy practices, please contact us by email.
