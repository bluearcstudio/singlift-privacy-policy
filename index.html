Vocal Stability Instrument

Measure voice steadiness in real time.

SingLift is a simple Android instrument for observing vocal stability while you sustain your voice. It is designed for focused long-tone practice and real-time voice steadiness feedback.

Privacy Policy Contact Support

SingLift uses microphone access for real-time measurement. Voice analysis is performed on your device.

Real-time feedback

Watch vocal stability respond as you sustain your voice.

Simple states

WEAK, FAIR, and STRONG make voice steadiness easy to read at a glance.

On-device processing

Voice measurement happens locally on your device. No account is required.

Built for focused voice practice.

SingLift is not a game or a singing score app. It is a focused vocal stability meter created to help users observe how steadily they can hold their voice during sustained vocal practice.
