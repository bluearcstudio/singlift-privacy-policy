Vocal Stability Instrument

A simple real-time tool for observing vocal steadiness.

SingLift helps you observe vocal stability in real time during focused voice practice. It is designed to be simple, calm, and privacy-friendly.

View Privacy Policy Read FAQ / Technical Note

Android app by Blue Arc Studio. Google Play listing is in preparation.

What it does

Observe voice steadiness in real time.

SingLift is not a singing score, diagnosis, or lesson service. It is a focused instrument for checking how steady your voice appears during a sustained vocal sound.

The app shows simple real-time feedback so you can observe changes in vocal steadiness while using your own voice.

Real-time feedback

See vocal steadiness feedback while you are using your voice.

Simple display

Designed around clear states, so you can focus on the sound itself.

Focused voice practice

Useful before or during personal voice practice when you want a calm reference.

Privacy-first design

No recording. No cloud upload.

SingLift is designed so that voice analysis happens on your device. The current version does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, user accounts, or cloud-based voice processing.

  • No voice recording
  • No voice storage
  • No cloud upload
  • No user account required
  • No advertising SDKs
  • Processed on your device
Purchase model

One-time purchase. No subscription.

Important note

SingLift is a simple observation tool.

SingLift does not provide medical advice, voice diagnosis, professional instruction, or a guarantee of vocal improvement.

It is intended as a simple real-time tool for observing vocal steadiness during focused voice practice.
